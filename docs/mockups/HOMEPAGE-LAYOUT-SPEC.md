## Canonical homepage, top to bottom

### 1. Header (white, logo overlaps the hero)

1. Circular Local Studies emblem, left, overlapping the photo by about half its height.
2. Wordmark: LOCAL STUDIES / GULF COUNTY, FL. Optional one-line tagline under it: Real data. Local stories. Stronger community. Brighter tomorrow.
3. Icon-plus-label nav, centered: Map, Explore, Data, Trackers, Events, Learn, About.
4. Dark search button (rounded square).
5. Sponsor / Advertise outline button, far right.

Do not keep the current cream sticky bar with text-only dropdowns as the home chrome. Dropdowns can still open under the icon labels.

### 2. LIVE NOW ticker (navy pill, sitting on the photo)

Left to right, live dots and short facts:

1. LIVE NOW or LIVE GULF COUNTY
2. Sunset or sunrise time
3. Turtle nests recorded today
4. Fishing bite (example: Red Snapper Good Bite)
5. Live music location
6. Beach flags
7. Air temp and sky

This replaces the current full-width dark conditions bar that sits under the hero.

### 3. Hero

1. Full-bleed cinematic photo. The photo is the page, not a banner over cream.
2. Soft left-to-transparent dark wash so white type stays readable. Do not use the current heavy forest overlay.
3. Script italic kicker (white). Pick one line: "Discover what's happening in" or "The best of" or "Your community. Your information."
4. Display headline, extra bold condensed, huge: GULF COUNTY, FL
5. Three or four short supporting lines. Current brand line is already close: Real data. Local stories. Stronger community. Brighter tomorrow.
6. Two CTAs only in the hero: Explore the Map (forest green, pin icon) and View Businesses (white pill, storefront icon). Sponsor moves to the header.

### 4. Four overlay cards (right of hero, glass white)

2 by 2. Each has an icon, title, one-line dek, and a chevron.

1. Community Data. Explore key insights and trends.
2. Events. See what's happening around town.
3. Business Directory. Support local businesses.
4. Live Trackers. Real-time updates that matter.

Optional upgrade from mockup 06: a faint photo or sparkline inside each card.

### 5. Stats band (navy or forest, overlapping the bottom of the hero)

Current homepage already has these four numbers. Move them out of the hero chart card and into this band. Drop the homepage economy chart.

### 6. What's Happening Right Now

Left: horizontal event cards with photo, status pill (LIVE, TONIGHT, THIS WEEKEND, date), title, time, location. Carousel arrows.

Right: Local Spotlight card with photo, short place story, Learn more.

### 7. Explore by Interest (icon row)

Beaches, Fishing, Nature, Food, Events, Family, History, Local Data, For Kids.

### 8. Tracker strip (four colored buttons)

1. Sea Turtle Tracker. Nesting season. Forest green.
2. Fishing Report. Today's bite. Navy.
3. Beach Conditions. Live updates. Gold / sand.
4. Weather Center. Local forecasts. Slate blue.

Preferred finish from mockup 08: water or sand texture inside each button, not a flat fill.

## Visual tokens to add

1. Navy family for ticker, stats band, and fishing button. Approx #0B1F3A, #0E2A47, #123056. Forest green stays for CTAs, turtle tracker, and brand.
2. Display type: extra-bold condensed sans for GULF COUNTY, FL. Current DM Sans is too round and too light for that lockup.
3. Script italic for the kicker. Need a second Google font (example: Great Vibes, Pinyon Script, or Playfair Display italic).
4. Overlay cards: white / 90% with blur, 16 to 24px radius, light shadow.

## What the live site does not match

1. Header is a cream civic bar, not a logo-over-photo tourism header with icon nav and Sponsor.
2. Hero is a left-copy plus dark stats plus chart stack, not a photo canvas with four glass cards.
3. Conditions sit in a dark strip under the hero, not as a LIVE ticker or photo pill.
4. No overlay quick-link cards.
5. Events are a static four-up with no LIVE badges, no carousel, no Local Spotlight.
6. Explore by category is six large editorial cards. Mockups use a compact icon row plus overlay cards.
7. Tracker entry is a simple list. Mockups use four branded color blocks.
8. Photography set is too small. Mockups need a dedicated hero plate plus event thumbs (pier sunset, live music, shrimp boats, farmers market, lighthouse, water tower, estuary).
9. All mockups are desktop. There is no mobile composition in the set. Phone layout has to be designed during build: ticker becomes a horizontal scroller, overlay cards stack under the headline, stats become a two-by-two, events become a snap carousel.
